🛡️ Pediatric Medication Safety & Storage Guidelines

Accurate Syringe Precision

Never use kitchen silverware spoons. Always use the milliliter (mL) calibrated oral syringe provided by our clinical pharmacy.

Weight vs Age Dosing

Pediatric drug metabolism relies strictly on body weight, not chronological age. Check with our triage line before altering any dose.

High-Up Locked Storage

Store all vitamins and liquid syrups in a cool, dry cabinet well out of reach and sight. Keep Poison Control handy: (800) 222-1222.
