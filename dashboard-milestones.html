💡 AAP Pediatric Guidance on Walking Timelines

Every child develops at their own unique pace. Independent walking comfortably occurs anytime between 9 and 15 months. Since Liam is actively cruising along furniture and pulling to stand with stability, his motor foundation is strong. Dr. Carter will evaluate his gait during the upcoming 14 October visit.

🧩 Motor & Sensory Games (12–15 Mo)

  • Stacking Soft Blocks: Encourages wrist rotation and spatial balance.
  • Push-Toy Cruising: Stable weighted carts reinforce ankle stability before independent steps.
  • Water Sensory Play: Pouring cups during bath time builds hand-eye coordination.

🗣️ Speech & Social Communication

  • Narrate Daily Routines: Name utensils, clothing items, and household sounds out loud.
  • Interactive Board Books: Lift-the-flap books encourage tactile pointing and animal sounds.
  • Mirror Expression Mimicry: Foster self-recognition and emotional bonding.
