Quick reference protocol to help you make informed decisions when your child is unwell.

Same-Day Outpatient Assessment

  • ✓ Fevers lasting 24-48 hours in children over 3 months
  • ✓ Ear pain, tugging, and suspected ear infections
  • ✓ Persistent barking cough or mild wheezing
  • ✓ Mild dehydration, reduced wet diapers
