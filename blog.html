Clinical Facts

Pediatric Mythbusters

Straight answers from our medical team on common internet advice.

Myth: “Fevers above 102°F will cause brain damage.”

Fact: Normal illness fevers regulated by the hypothalamus do not cause brain injury. Fever is simply the body's immune defense producing antibodies. We treat discomfort, not just numbers.

Myth: “Hard-sole high-top shoes help toddlers learn to walk better.”

Fact: Barefoot walking is ideal for foot development! Feeling textures with toes builds foot arch musculature and neuromuscular balance pathways.

Myth: “Teething causes high fevers and severe vomiting.”

Fact: Teething may cause drooling and slight low-grade warmth (under 100.4°F), but true fevers indicate a viral or bacterial exposure that warrants evaluation.
