Clinical Programs

Comprehensive Medical Specializations

Detailed pediatric programs designed for every stage of early infancy through adolescence.

0 – 12 Months

Neonatal & Early Infancy Care

Gentle transition care from hospital discharge. Covers hyperbilirubinemia (jaundice) non-invasive monitoring, weight gain curves, umbilical care, and infant autonomic stability.

Diagnostic Protocol: Transcutaneous Bilirubinometer, WHO Growth Metrics
Schedule Neonatal Exam →
All Ages

Pediatric Cardiology & Murmurs

Led by Dr. Julian Reed, MD, PhD. Preventive cardiology auscultations, evaluation of innocent vs organic pediatric heart murmurs, exercise tolerance, and athletic clearances.

Diagnostic Protocol: Pediatric 12-Lead ECG, Doppler Auscultation
Book Screening with Dr. Reed →
6 Mo – 18 Yrs

Pediatric Allergy & Asthma Suite

Early food allergy introduction protocols, childhood eczema flare treatments, seasonal rhinitis, and customized asthma action plans for home and daycare.

Diagnostic Protocol: Skin Prick Tests, Pediatric Spirometry
Request Allergy Consultation →
12 Mo – 6 Yrs

Neurodevelopmental & Speech

Comprehensive multi-disciplinary evaluations for speech delays, sensory processing, fine/gross motor milestones, and early socialization intervention.

Diagnostic Protocol: ASQ-3 Standard, M-CHAT-R/F Screening
Schedule Developmental Review →
Infants to Teens

Pediatric Nutrition & Metabolic Care

Nutritional therapy for failure to thrive, iron deficiency, gluten sensitivity, selective eating behaviors, and balanced metabolic growth plans.

Diagnostic Protocol: Micro-sample Iron / Ferritin Screening, Body Composition
Book Nutrition Visit →

Pediatric Urgent Care & Rapid Lab

Rapid diagnosis for strep, RSV, flu, ear infections, dehydration, minor lacerations, and acute wheezing with on-site pharmacy dispensing.

Purpose-Built Architecture

A Clinical Facility Designed for Children’s Senses

Unlike austere hospital corridors, our clinic incorporates curved organic arches, non-flicker natural lighting, sound-dampening acoustic cork flooring, and dedicated well-child vs sick-child separate entries.

1
Dual-Circulation Air Purification

Medical-grade HEPA 14 filters with negative pressure suites preventing airborne cross-exposure.

2
Tactile Sensory Waiting Nooks

Engaging wooden Montessori blocks, sensory balance beams, and calming soft color zones.

3
Warm-Touch Medical Equipment

Stethoscope warmers, animal-shaped pulse oximeters, and distraction toys during physical exams.
